Jingsen Zhang 张景森

Ph.D. Student @ Renmin University of China
📧 zhangjingsen@ruc.edu.cn

I am a fourth-year Ph.D. student at Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence, Renmin University of China, supervised by Prof. Xu Chen. Prior to this, I earned my Master's degree at the School of Information, Renmin University of China, supervised by Prof. Wayne Xin Zhao.

My research interests lie in Recommender Systems, with a particular focus on Explainable Recommendation. Recently, I have been exploring the field of LLM-based Agents.
